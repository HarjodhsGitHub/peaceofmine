Chrome / G27 troubleshooting
        
Use HTTPS or localhost. For a remote rover, connect the wheel to the computer running Chrome. USB visibility (including 046d:c294) does not prove that the OS driver or browser exposes its controls. If no device appears, check the OS game-controller panel, USB passthrough if using a VM, and try another USB port. Non-standard wheels need manual mapping here. Force feedback and raw WebHID driving are not implemented.

Position units and calibration help
              
The angle is the servo shaft position, not its angle to the ground. 4096 raw ticks = 360°. Left decreases the angle; mounting may reverse physical direction.

              
With clear space and the linkage supported, jog to a safe position, release, and record it below. Leave clearance from mechanical stops. Recording does not move the servo.

              
RC override is allowed for the arm. Vehicle driving is blocked during calibration. Selecting another servo requires website controls to be disarmed.
